\section{Collision Avoidance Validation}

The mathematical guarantee of collision-free operation is paramount in multi-robot cells. The \texttt{ZoneDetectionManager} and the MoveIt Task Constructor (MTC) Safe Resolution pipeline were subjected to rigorous edge-case testing to validate their efficacy.

\subsection{Zone Manager and Spatial Tracking Accuracy}

\subsection{Edge Case Scenarios and MTC Resolution}

The algorithmic fail-safe was validated by intentionally commanding both arms to reach conflicting coordinates within the shared interference zone simultaneously. The performance of the MTC resolution pipeline is measured by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Detection Latency ($L_{detect}$):} The time taken (in milliseconds) from breaching the approach margin to the issuance of the \texttt{COLLISION\_WARNING} interrupt.
    \item \textbf{Resolution Computation Time ($T_{res}$):} The time required for the MTC \texttt{PipelinePlanner} to calculate a valid 12-DOF escape trajectory.
    \item \textbf{Resolution Success Rate ($SR_{res}$):} The percentage of imminent collision events successfully resolved without requiring a hard system abort.
\end{itemize}
