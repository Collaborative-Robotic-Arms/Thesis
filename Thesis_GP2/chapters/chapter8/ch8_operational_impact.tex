\section{Operational Impact and Human Factors}

The transition from a laboratory prototype to a commercial factory floor profoundly alters the human-machine dynamic. Instead of outright labor replacement, the integration of collaborative robotic cells shifts the human workforce toward supervisory roles, specifically removing them from dull, dirty, and dangerous manual tasks.

\subsection{Ergonomics and Safety}
By delegating repetitive material handling and precise positioning to the dual-arm system, operators are relieved of severe ergonomic strain. Industrial pilot programs replacing direct part handling with cobot supervision have reported a 30\% reduction in ergonomic incidents within three months \cite{szghtech2026cobot}. This transition directly improves job satisfaction, reduces physical fatigue by the end of shifts, and significantly lowers worker compensation costs.

\subsection{Workforce Upskilling}
The deployment of a Digital-Twin-enabled cell transforms the operator's role into that of a "System Manager". Workers transition from manual assembly to managing the logic, overseeing the IoT dashboard, and performing advanced Quality Assurance (QA). This necessitates targeted training programs to ensure the workforce can confidently interact with ROS 2 interfaces, reset MTC pipelines, and interpret Digital Twin telemetry.
